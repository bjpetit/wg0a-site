WG0A Ham Radio Software

This site collects practical notes and setup guides for running ham radio software.

Dire Wolf

Dire Wolf is a software TNC and APRS modem. Content here will cover installation, audio configuration, and on-air testing.

ARDOPC

ARDOPC provides ARDOP modem functionality for digital HF work. Content here will cover basic setup and interoperability tips.

LinBPQ

LinBPQ is a packet/BBS/node suite for Linux. Content here will cover first-run setup, configuration, and service operation.
